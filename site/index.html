Novel evidence for urolithin A in regulating human platelet signaling and aggregationUrolithin A 調控人類血小板訊號傳遞與凝集之新證據

Urolithin A (UA), a gut microbial metabolite of pomegranate polyphenols, was investigated for its effects on human platelet activation. UA attenuated platelet aggregation, granule secretion (P-selectin, ATP), reactive oxygen species production, calcium mobilization, and Akt phosphorylation induced by arachidonic acid and collagen. UA also inhibited outside-in signaling, including platelet adhesion, spreading, and clot retraction. In a rat FeCl₃-induced thrombosis model, UA prolonged the time to arterial occlusion. These findings identify UA as a potential antiplatelet agent acting through the PI3K/Akt pathway.本研究探討石榴多酚腸道代謝物 urolithin A（UA）對人類血小板活化的影響。UA 抑制花生四烯酸及膠原蛋白誘導的血小板凝集、顆粒分泌（P-selectin、ATP）、活性氧生成、鈣離子動員及 Akt 磷酸化，並抑制 outside-in 訊號（血小板黏附、伸展、血塊回縮）。在大鼠 FeCl₃ 動脈血栓模型中，UA 延長血管阻塞時間。研究結果顯示 UA 可能透過 PI3K/Akt 路徑發揮抗血小板作用。

02

Current · Ph.D. research進行中 · 博士研究

Fucoidan from Saccharina japonica in platelet activation and burn wound healing褐藻醣膠（Saccharina japonica fucoidan）調控血小板活化與燒燙傷傷口癒合

This project investigates how Saccharina japonica fucoidan (SJF) modulates platelet activation and its potential role in promoting burn wound healing. Using flow cytometry, western blotting, and phosphoproteomic profiling, the study traces signaling events downstream of fucoidan–platelet interactions.本計畫研究日本海帶褐藻醣膠（SJF）如何調控血小板活化，及其在促進燒燙傷傷口癒合中的潛在角色。運用流式細胞術、西方墨點法與磷酸蛋白質體學分析 fucoidan 與血小板交互作用的下游訊號事件。
