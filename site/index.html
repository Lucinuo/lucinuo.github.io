Research研究主題

My doctoral work investigates how fucoidan—a sulfated polysaccharide isolated from the brown alga Saccharina japonica—modulates early platelet activation in the setting of burn wound healing. Platelets are among the first cellular responders to vascular injury, and their activation state critically shapes downstream hemostasis, inflammatory signaling, and tissue repair. Using biochemical and cell-based approaches, I examine the receptor-level mechanisms by which S. japonica-derived fucoidan (SJF) influences platelet surface receptor expression and intracellular signaling cascades, with the broader aim of characterizing marine-derived bioactive compounds as candidate adjuvants for improving outcomes in thermally injured tissue.

我的博士研究探討來自褐藻（Saccharina japonica）的硫酸化多醣—— 褐藻醣膠（SJF）——如何調控燒燙傷傷口癒合過程中的早期血小板活化。 血小板是血管損傷後最早的細胞反應者之一，其活化狀態對後續的止血、 炎症訊號傳遞與組織修復均有深遠影響。本研究以生化分析與細胞實驗為主要平台， 探討 SJF 調控血小板表面受體表現及胞內訊號傳遞的分子機制， 以評估海洋天然化合物作為改善燒燙傷癒合輔助劑的潛力。

About關於

I am a third-year PhD candidate in the Graduate Institute of Life Sciences at National Defense Medical University (NDMU), Taiwan, supervised by Prof. Chih-Chin Shih. My research sits at the intersection of vascular biology, pharmacology, and natural product science.

我是國防醫學大學生命科學研究所博士三年級生，指導教授為施志勤教授。 研究橫跨血管生物學、藥理學與天然物科學三個領域。
