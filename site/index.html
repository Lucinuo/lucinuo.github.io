Demonstration · Research Storytelling示範 · 研究敘事

This section is a demonstration of how the site presents a mechanistic research narrative. The example shown — tumor-associated macrophage metabolism in hepatocellular carcinoma — is drawn from published cancer-immunometabolism literature and is not the author's own research.

本區為此網站「機制敘事」呈現格式的示範。所示範例——肝細胞癌中腫瘤相關巨噬細胞的代謝重編程——取自已發表的腫瘤免疫代謝文獻，並非作者本人的研究。

This walkthrough follows how metabolic reprogramming of tumor-associated macrophages (TAMs) shapes the immunosuppressive microenvironment in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). Macrophages within the tumor stroma undergo a spectrum of functional polarization states that govern local cytokine networks, immune cell exclusion, and resistance to therapy. Using primary macrophage culture systems, co-culture models, and metabolic profiling, it traces how HIF-1α-mediated glycolytic reprogramming and mTOR signaling converge to sustain protumoral macrophage phenotypes—with the aim of identifying metabolic vulnerabilities that could be exploited to remodel the HCC immune microenvironment.

此範例呈現腫瘤相關巨噬細胞（TAM）的代謝重編程如何塑造肝細胞癌（HCC）的免疫抑制微環境。 腫瘤基質中的巨噬細胞在功能極化過程中調控局部細胞激素網絡、免疫細胞排斥及治療抗性。 透過初代巨噬細胞培養、共培養模型與代謝表型分析，並追溯 HIF-1α 介導的糖解重編程 與 mTOR 訊號如何協同維持促腫瘤巨噬細胞表現型，以識別可用於重塑 HCC 免疫微環境的代謝脆弱點。

Step 01步驟 01

Macrophages in the HCC stroma巨噬細胞在 HCC 基質裡

Tumor-associated macrophages (TAMs) reside within the HCC tumor stroma, where they help shape the local immune microenvironment. 腫瘤相關巨噬細胞（TAM）位於肝細胞癌的腫瘤基質中，是塑造免疫微環境的關鍵細胞。

Step 02步驟 02

Hypoxia drives HIF-1α glycolytic reprogramming缺氧驅動 HIF-1α 糖解重編程

Hypoxia induces HIF-1α-mediated glycolytic reprogramming in TAMs, shifting their functional state. 缺氧微環境誘導 HIF-1α，驅動 TAM 的糖解代謝重編程，改變其功能狀態。

Step 03步驟 03

mTOR sustains protumoral macrophage phenotypesmTOR 協同維持促腫瘤表現型

mTOR signaling converges with glycolytic reprogramming to sustain protumoral macrophage phenotypes. mTOR 訊號與糖解重編程協同，維持促腫瘤的巨噬細胞表現型。

Step 04步驟 04

Immune suppression leads to therapy resistance免疫抑制微環境 → 治療抗性

Reprogrammed TAMs support cytokine networks, immune cell exclusion, tumor progression, and resistance to therapy. 重編程後的 TAM 形成免疫抑制微環境，助長腫瘤進展與對治療的抗性。

Step 05步驟 05

Target metabolic vulnerabilities to remodel the microenvironment鎖定代謝弱點重塑微環境

The goal is to identify metabolic vulnerabilities that can be exploited to remodel the HCC immune microenvironment. 目標是識別這些代謝弱點，作為重塑 HCC 免疫微環境的介入點。
