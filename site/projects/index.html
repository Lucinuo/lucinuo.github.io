Open implementation公開實作

Lucinuo Website System

A public digital studio connecting research context, formal outputs, selected projects, and open development records.連結研究脈絡、正式成果、精選專案與公開開發紀錄的個人數位工作室。

What內容
A bilingual, responsive public website with a deliberately separate private-workspace boundary.具備雙語響應式體驗，並明確分隔私人工作區的公開網站。
Implementation技術實作
Semantic HTML, shared CSS and JavaScript, GitHub Actions, and GitHub Pages—with no framework or build step.使用語意化 HTML、共用 CSS 與 JavaScript、GitHub Actions 與 GitHub Pages，不依賴框架或建置流程。
Role負責項目
Information architecture, content strategy, interface design, implementation, and maintenance.資訊架構、內容策略、介面設計、技術實作與維護。
Problem問題
Research, publications, projects, source code, and personal tools need different levels of context and access.研究、文獻、專案、原始碼與個人工具需要不同的說明脈絡與存取權限。
Key contribution主要貢獻
One design language, clear public content ownership, responsive layouts, and an explicit privacy boundary.建立一致設計語言、清楚的公開內容分工、響應式版面與明確隱私邊界。
Outcome成果
A maintainable system where Lucinuo explains the work and GitHub documents public implementation.形成可長期維護的系統：Lucinuo 解釋工作，GitHub 記錄公開實作。

02 · Completed case study · 202602 · 完成案例 · 2026

Literature Knowledge System

A literature workflow that makes a large source collection traceable, comparable, and useful for careful evidence synthesis.讓大量研究來源可以追溯、比較，並支援審慎證據整理的文獻工作流程。

What內容
A private source-to-note workflow described here only at the level of its reusable method.私人使用的來源到筆記流程；公開頁面只說明可重用的方法。
Role負責項目
Workflow design, evidence boundaries, note structure, and completion audit.流程設計、證據邊界、筆記結構與完成稽核。
Problem問題
A growing literature collection becomes difficult to search and easy to overinterpret.文獻量增加後難以搜尋，也容易把間接證據誤讀成完整機制。
Key contribution主要貢獻
A traceable boundary between source text, interpretation, mechanism claims, and unresolved questions.清楚分隔來源原文、推論、機制主張與尚未解決的問題。
Outcome成果
A repeatable method for turning sources into structured, auditable knowledge without exposing private research content.在不公開私人研究內容的前提下，將來源整理成結構化、可稽核知識的可重複方法。

Public access: method summary only. Research identity, source materials, counts, and private records are not public.公開範圍：僅方法摘要。研究身分、來源檔案、數量與私人紀錄均不公開。

03 · Experimental · 202603 · 實驗性 · 2026

Research Information Router研究資訊分流器

A lightweight decision aid for choosing where a piece of research information belongs before it disappears into the wrong system.在研究資訊被放進錯誤系統之前，協助判斷它真正應該去哪裡的輕量工具。

What內容
A question-based router for sources, interpretations, raw records, decisions, tasks, and files.針對來源、推論、原始紀錄、研究決策、任務與檔案的問答式分流。
Role負責項目
Decision-rule design and interaction structure.判斷規則與互動結構設計。
Problem問題
Different information states were being mixed across sources, notes, tasks, and raw files.不同狀態的資訊容易混在來源、筆記、任務與原始檔案中。
Key contribution主要貢獻
A short sequence of boundary questions instead of another filing dashboard.以短而清楚的邊界問題取代另一套檔案管理儀表板。
Outcome成果
A clear destination, a boundary, and one next handling step.每次得到一個清楚去向、一條邊界與一個處理步驟。

Public access: case study only while the decision rules are being validated.公開範圍：規則仍在驗證中，目前僅提供案例說明。

04 · Experimental · 202504 · 實驗性 · 2025

Presentation demonstration呈現方式示範

Mechanism Research Storytelling機制研究敘事

An editorial demonstration for presenting a published biological mechanism one evidence node at a time. It is not a statement of Lucille Huang's own research.逐一呈現已發表生物機制證據節點的編輯示範，並非 Lucille Huang 本人的研究內容。

What內容
A stepwise HCC/TAM mechanism presentation demonstration.逐步呈現 HCC／TAM 機制的示範。
Role負責項目
Research narrative, visual hierarchy, and interaction design.研究敘事、視覺層級與互動設計。
Problem問題
Dense pathway diagrams often hide evidence boundaries and overwhelm the reader.密集路徑圖容易掩蓋證據邊界，也讓讀者無法掌握主線。
Key contribution主要貢獻
A paced editorial sequence that keeps presentation examples separate from personal research claims.以有節奏的編輯順序，清楚分隔呈現示範與個人研究主張。
Outcome成果
A readable sequence with an explicit demonstration disclaimer.一套可依序閱讀，且明確標示示範性質的呈現方式。
