01 · Featured · Active

Bearing

See clearly. Move deliberately.

A personal system for reflection, direction, and action.一套用來反思、判斷方向並形成行動的個人系統。

What
A focused web tool for turning one honest observation into one next move.把一個誠實觀察整理成一個下一步的專注型網頁工具。
Role
Product definition, information architecture, interaction design, and long-term use.產品定義、資訊架構、互動設計與長期實際使用。
Problem
Reflection tools often become trackers, dashboards, or another system to maintain.反思工具經常變成追蹤器、儀表板，或另一套需要維護的系統。
Key contribution
A quiet four-step loop that keeps one question and one decision in focus.以安靜的四步循環，讓使用者一次只專注一個問題與一個判斷。
Outcome
Notice, Choose, Reorient, Move — with device-local records and compatible legacy imports.Notice、Choose、Reorient、Move，並保留裝置端紀錄與舊資料相容能力。

02 · Active

Open implementation公開實作

Lucinuo Website System

The shared information and design system connecting Lucinuo, Bearing, and the public development record.連結 Lucinuo、Bearing 與公開開發紀錄的共用資訊與設計系統。

What
A bilingual, responsive website and installable Bearing web app under one GitHub Pages domain.在同一個 GitHub Pages 網域下運作的雙語響應式網站與可安裝 Bearing 網頁 App。
Role
Brand architecture, content strategy, interface design, implementation, and maintenance.品牌架構、內容策略、介面設計、技術實作與維護。
Problem
The personal site, product, and source code previously read as separate, inconsistent destinations.原本的個人網站、產品與原始碼像三個互不連貫的入口。
Key contribution
One design language, clear content ownership, Apple-device layouts, and a safe route and data migration.建立一致設計語言、清楚內容分工、Apple 裝置版面，以及安全的網址與資料遷移。
Outcome
A maintainable public system where the website explains the work and GitHub shows how it is built.形成可長期維護的公開系統：網站解釋工作，GitHub 呈現如何實作。

03 · Completed case study

SJF Literature Knowledge System

A literature workflow that makes a large source collection traceable, comparable, and useful for mechanism building.讓大量來源可以追溯、比較，並真正支援機制整理的文獻工作流程。

What
A complete source-to-note workflow for 176 SJF reference PDFs.涵蓋 176 篇 SJF 參考 PDF 的完整來源到筆記流程。
Role
Workflow design, evidence boundaries, note structure, and completion audit.流程設計、證據邊界、筆記結構與完成稽核。
Problem
A growing literature collection becomes difficult to search and easy to overinterpret.文獻量增加後難以搜尋，也容易把間接證據誤讀成完整機制。
Key contribution
A traceable boundary between source text, interpretation, mechanism claims, and unresolved questions.清楚分隔來源原文、推論、機制主張與尚未解決的問題。
Outcome
176 readable sources, Traditional Chinese source notes, and an auditable knowledge map.176 篇可讀來源、繁體中文來源筆記，以及可稽核的知識地圖。

Public access: case study only. Source materials and private research records are not public.公開範圍：僅案例說明。來源檔案與私人研究紀錄不公開。

04 · Experimental

Research Information Router研究資訊分流器

A lightweight decision aid for choosing where a piece of research information belongs before it disappears into the wrong system.在研究資訊被放進錯誤系統之前，協助判斷它真正應該去哪裡的輕量工具。

What
A question-based router for sources, interpretations, raw records, decisions, tasks, and files.針對來源、推論、原始紀錄、研究決策、任務與檔案的問答式分流。
Role
Decision-rule design and interaction structure.判斷規則與互動結構設計。
Problem
Different information states were being mixed across sources, notes, tasks, and raw files.不同狀態的資訊容易混在來源、筆記、任務與原始檔案中。
Key contribution
A short sequence of boundary questions instead of another filing dashboard.以短而清楚的邊界問題取代另一套檔案管理儀表板。
Outcome
A clear destination, a boundary, and one next handling step.每次得到一個清楚去向、一條邊界與一個處理步驟。

Public access: case study only while the decision rules are being validated.公開範圍：規則仍在驗證中，目前僅提供案例說明。

05 · Experimental

Mechanism Research Storytelling機制研究敘事

An editorial method for presenting a biological mechanism one evidence node at a time.一次呈現一個證據節點的生物機制編輯方法。

What
A stepwise HCC/TAM mechanism presentation demonstration.逐步呈現 HCC／TAM 機制的示範。
Role
Research narrative, visual hierarchy, and interaction design.研究敘事、視覺層級與互動設計。
Problem
Dense pathway diagrams often hide evidence boundaries and overwhelm the reader.密集路徑圖容易掩蓋證據邊界，也讓讀者無法掌握主線。
Key contribution
A paced editorial sequence that keeps presentation examples separate from personal research claims.以有節奏的編輯順序，清楚分隔呈現示範與個人研究主張。
Outcome
A readable sequence with an explicit demonstration disclaimer.一套可依序閱讀，且明確標示示範性質的呈現方式。
