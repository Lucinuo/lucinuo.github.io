Projects專案

Tools shaped by real work.從真實工作中長出的工具。

Each project begins with a concrete problem: too much information, an unclear decision, or a workflow that asks for more maintenance than it returns.每個專案都從具體問題開始：資訊太多、判斷不清楚，或一套維護成本大於回報的工作流程。

01 · Product

Bearing

See clearly. Move deliberately.

A personal system for reflection, direction, and action.一套用來反思、判斷方向並形成行動的個人系統。

What
A focused web tool for turning one honest observation into one next move.把一個誠實觀察整理成一個下一步的專注型網頁工具。
Role
Product definition, information architecture, interaction design, and long-term use.產品定義、資訊架構、互動設計與長期實際使用。
Problem
Reflection tools often become trackers, dashboards, or another system to maintain.反思工具經常變成追蹤器、儀表板，或另一套需要維護的系統。
Outcome
A quiet loop: Notice, Choose, Reorient, Move.一個安靜的循環：Notice、Choose、Reorient、Move。

02 · Research workflow

SJF Literature Knowledge System

A literature workflow that makes a large source collection traceable, comparable, and useful for mechanism building.讓大量來源可以追溯、比較，並真正支援機制整理的文獻工作流程。

What
A complete source-to-note workflow for 176 SJF reference PDFs.涵蓋 176 篇 SJF 參考 PDF 的完整來源到筆記流程。
Role
Workflow design, evidence boundaries, note structure, and completion audit.流程設計、證據邊界、筆記結構與完成稽核。
Problem
A growing literature collection becomes difficult to search and easy to overinterpret.文獻量增加後難以搜尋，也容易把間接證據誤讀成完整機制。
Outcome
176 readable sources, Traditional Chinese source notes, and an auditable knowledge map.176 篇可讀來源、繁體中文來源筆記，以及可稽核的知識地圖。

03 · Decision tool

Research Information Router研究資訊分流器

A lightweight decision aid for choosing where a piece of research information belongs before it disappears into the wrong system.在研究資訊被放進錯誤系統之前，協助判斷它真正應該去哪裡的輕量工具。

What
A question-based router for sources, interpretations, raw records, decisions, tasks, and files.針對來源、推論、原始紀錄、研究決策、任務與檔案的問答式分流。
Role
Decision-rule design and interaction structure.判斷規則與互動結構設計。
Problem
Different information states were being mixed across Zotero, notes, tasks, and raw files.不同狀態的資訊容易混在 Zotero、筆記、任務與原始檔案中。
Outcome
A clear destination, a boundary, and one next handling step.每次得到一個清楚去向、一條邊界與一個處理步驟。

04 · Research visualization

Mechanism Research Storytelling機制研究敘事

An editorial method for presenting a biological mechanism one evidence node at a time.一次呈現一個證據節點的生物機制編輯方法。

What
A stepwise HCC/TAM mechanism presentation demonstration.逐步呈現 HCC／TAM 機制的示範。
Role
Research narrative, visual hierarchy, and interaction design.研究敘事、視覺層級與互動設計。
Problem
Dense pathway diagrams often hide evidence boundaries and overwhelm the reader.密集路徑圖容易掩蓋證據邊界，也讓讀者無法掌握主線。
Outcome
A readable sequence with an explicit demonstration disclaimer.一套可依序閱讀，且明確標示示範性質的呈現方式。
