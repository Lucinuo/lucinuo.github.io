Bearing

A private space to notice where I am, work out what matters, and find one honest next step. This page explains the concept; the private login stays outside the public site.一個私人空間，用來看見自己在哪裡、想清楚什麼重要，然後找到一個誠實的下一步。這一頁只說明概念，私人登入放在公開網站之外。

The idea核心概念

Reflection without performance.反思，不需要表演。

Bearing isn’t a productivity product, a habit tracker, or a score for personal growth. It’s just something I use to cut the noise and think a few personal decisions through carefully.Bearing 不是生產力產品、習慣追蹤器，也不是替個人成長打分數的東西。它就是我自己用的，用來降低雜訊、把少數幾個個人決定想清楚。

What內容
A self-use application for reflection, direction, private records, and next-step planning.自用的反思、方向、私人紀錄與下一步規劃系統。
Public scope公開範圍
The design problem, principles, information structure, and concept visual.設計問題、原則、資訊架構與概念視覺。
Private scope私人範圍
The working application, private login, authentication, records, sync, backups, and all personal data.實際應用程式、私人登入、身份驗證、紀錄、同步、備份與所有個人資料。
Role負責項目
Product framing, information architecture, interaction design, the interface, and the privacy boundary—all mine.產品定位、資訊架構、互動設計、介面和隱私邊界，都是我自己來。

Design principles設計原則

Less management. More clarity.少一點管理，多一點清楚。

01

See before scoring先看見，不先評分

The system begins with context and language, not charts, streaks, or performance scores.系統從脈絡與文字開始，而不是圖表、連續紀錄或績效分數。

02

One question at a time一次只處理一個問題

Information unfolds gradually so reflection does not become another administrative burden.資訊逐步展開，避免反思變成另一項管理負擔。

03

Private by structure從結構上保持私人

The public website can explain the thinking, but the working application is kept behind real authentication and private storage.公開網站可以說明設計思考；真正的應用程式則必須放在真實身份驗證與私人儲存之後。
