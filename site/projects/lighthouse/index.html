Lighthouse

A public concept prototype for a companionship app that says “I’m here” without demanding an immediate reply.陪伴應用程式的公開概念原型：用一種安靜的方式說「我在這裡」，而不要求對方立刻回應。

Open the concept開啟概念展示All projects所有專案

The idea核心概念

Presence without pressure.陪伴，但不施加壓力。

Many companionship and communication tools turn closeness into alerts, read receipts, and exact timestamps. Lighthouse removes most of that interface. One person leaves a light on. The other can notice it when they are ready.許多陪伴與溝通工具把親近變成通知、已讀與精確時間。Lighthouse 拿掉大部分介面：一個人把燈點亮，另一個人在準備好時自然看見。

What內容

Status狀態

Interaction principles互動原則

Designed to wait well.能夠安靜等待的設計。

01

No precise clock沒有精確時鐘

“A little while ago” communicates presence without turning time into a measure of care.「不久之前」能傳達陪伴，又不會讓時間變成衡量在意程度的工具。

02

No escalating notification沒有不斷升級的通知

The scene remains calm when nobody responds. Nothing flashes, counts, or asks for attention.即使沒有人回應，畫面仍保持平靜；沒有閃爍、計數或催促。

03

Warmth has one job暖色只做一件事

The amber beam appears only when the lamp is on, so color itself carries the meaning.琥珀光只在點燈後出現，讓顏色本身承擔訊息。
