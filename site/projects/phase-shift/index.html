Phase Shift

Clear the field. Change the phase. A competitive falling-block puzzle game where pressure becomes a choice: attack now, or make room to recover.清空場面，改變相位。一款競技方塊遊戲：累積的壓力最後會變成選擇——現在進攻，或替自己爭取喘息空間。

The game遊戲內容

One field. One opponent. One deliberate shift.一個盤面、一個對手，以及一次有意識的轉換。

Arrange the falling pieces, clear complete rows, build combos, and send interference to the computer opponent. Every clear also charges the Phase meter. When it is ready, the same energy can become an attack or a guard.排列落下的方塊、消除完整橫列、累積連擊，並把干擾送到電腦對手的盤面。每次消除也會累積相位能量；能量充滿時，同一股力量可以拿來進攻，也可以拿來防守。

Opponent對手
A computer player that follows the same field, interference, and phase rules.遵守相同盤面、干擾與相位規則的電腦對手。
Shift Attack相位進攻
The next successful clear sends two additional interference rows.下一次成功消除時，額外送出兩列干擾。
Shift Guard相位防守
Remove up to two interference rows and briefly slow the falling speed.移除最多兩列干擾，並短暫降低方塊下落速度。
Controls操作
Keyboard on desktop and a dedicated touch layout on mobile.電腦使用鍵盤；手機使用重新安排過的觸控操作。
Role負責項目
Game concept, rules, visual direction, interface, computer opponent, and front-end implementation.遊戲概念、規則、美術方向、介面、電腦對手與前端實作。

Design principles設計原則

Quiet until the moment matters.平常安靜，重要時刻才發光。

01

Pressure stays readable壓力要看得懂

Incoming interference, field height, and Phase readiness are visible without alerts covering the board.即將到來的干擾、盤面高度與相位狀態都看得到，但不會用警示遮住遊戲。

02

Energy has one color能量只用一種顏色

Silver carries the pieces. Amber appears only for combos, attacks, and a ready Phase meter.銀灰代表方塊；琥珀色只出現在連擊、攻擊與相位充滿的時刻。

03

The same rules for both sides雙方遵守相同規則

The computer evaluates the field, but it does not receive hidden pieces or immunity from interference.電腦會判斷盤面，但不會偷拿未公開的方塊，也不會免疫干擾。
