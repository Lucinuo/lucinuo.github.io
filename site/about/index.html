Lucinuo is how I bring research, building, and reflection into one practice.Lucinuo 讓研究、建造與反思成為同一個實踐。

Lucille Huang · 黃詩婷

Researcher, builder, and the person behind Lucinuo.研究者、建造者，也是 Lucinuo 背後的人。

My training is in life science and pharmacology. My work extends from experimental biology and evidence synthesis to research visualization, AI-assisted workflows, and personal systems for clearer thinking.我的訓練背景是生命科學與藥理學，工作從實驗生物學與證據整理，延伸到研究視覺化、AI 輔助工作流程，以及幫助清楚思考的個人系統。

I am currently pursuing a PhD at the Graduate Institute of Life Sciences, National Defense Medical University in Taiwan. The degree is part of my academic context; Lucinuo is the broader practice that holds the research, tools, and ways of thinking together.我目前就讀於國防醫學大學生命科學研究所博士班。學位是學術背景的一部分；Lucinuo 則是承接研究、工具與思考方法的更完整實踐。
