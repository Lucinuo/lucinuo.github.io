About關於

A practice shaped by questions, evidence, and making things useful.一個由問題、證據與實際成果形塑的實踐。

Lucille Huang · 黃詩婷

I work between biological research and practical systems.我在生物醫學研究與實用系統之間工作。

My work begins with biological questions and careful evidence. It extends into scientific communication, research visualization, AI-assisted workflows, and tools that help complex information become usable.我的工作從生物醫學問題與審慎證據開始，延伸到科學溝通、研究視覺化、AI 輔助工作流程，以及讓複雜資訊真正可用的工具。

Lucinuo is the independent practice that holds these parts together. It shows not only what I study or build, but how I observe a problem, give it structure, make something useful, and revise it through reflection.Lucinuo 是承接這些工作的獨立實踐。它不只呈現我研究或建立了什麼，也呈現我如何觀察問題、整理結構、做出可用成果，再透過反思修正。

The practice實踐方式

Research, build, and grow are parts of one method.研究、建造與成長，是同一套方法的不同階段。

01

Research研究

Experimental biology, evidence synthesis, and scientific reasoning used to understand what is actually happening.以實驗生物學、證據整理與科學推理，理解真正正在發生的事。

02

Build建造

Research visualization, AI-assisted workflows, websites, and knowledge systems that turn understanding into usable form.以研究視覺化、AI 輔助工作流程、網站與知識系統，把理解轉成可以使用的形式。

03

Grow成長

Reflection that treats results and change as information for deciding what matters next.把結果與改變視為資訊，透過反思判斷接下來真正重要的事。

Background背景

Academic context, kept in context.學術背景，是這個實踐的一部分。

My academic training provides the scientific depth behind the work. It is important context, but not the complete definition of Lucinuo.學術訓練提供這些工作的科學深度。它是重要背景，但不是 Lucinuo 的全部定義。
