Ex vivo human platelets離體人類血小板

Research story

01 · The question研究問題

Platelets protect us from bleeding—but excessive activation can obstruct blood flow.血小板能止血，但過度活化也可能阻礙血流。

The study asked whether urolithin A, a gut microbial metabolite of ellagitannins, changes how isolated human platelets respond.本研究想知道：鞣花單寧的腸道菌代謝物 urolithin A，是否會改變離體人類血小板的反應。

02 · Activation活化

Arachidonic acid or collagen switches the platelets into an active state.花生四烯酸或膠原蛋白，會讓血小板進入活化狀態。

They aggregate, release ATP, expose activation markers, and spread across fibrinogen-coated surfaces.血小板開始凝集、釋放 ATP、表現活化標誌，並在纖維蛋白原表面伸展。

03 · Intervention介入

Urolithin A enters the experiment before platelet stimulation.刺激血小板前，先加入 urolithin A。

Responses were compared across defined experimental concentrations—not after eating a food or taking a supplement.研究比較的是明確實驗濃度下的反應，不是吃下食物或補充品後的效果。

04 · Observed response觀察結果

Several activation responses became smaller.多項血小板活化反應下降。

At specific concentrations, UA reduced aggregation, ATP release, P-selectin expression, adhesion, spreading, clot retraction, oxidative signal, and Akt Ser473 phosphorylation.在特定濃度下，UA 降低凝集、ATP 釋放、P-selectin 表現、黏附、伸展、血塊回縮、氧化訊號與 Akt Ser473 磷酸化。

05 · The honest conclusion證據邊界

UA modulated platelet responses in these ex vivo experiments.UA 在這些離體實驗中調節了血小板反應。

That supports a laboratory finding. It does not establish an oral effect, clinical benefit, therapeutic use, or human safety.這支持的是實驗室觀察；不能直接推論口服效果、臨床效益、治療用途或人體安全性。

Evidence layer證據層

Open the measurements behind the story.展開查看故事背後的實驗量測。

Aggregation and ATP release凝集與 ATP 釋放＋

UA (3, 10, and 30 μM) reduced responses induced by arachidonic acid or collagen in washed human platelets. Thesis Figures 6–9.在人類洗滌血小板中，UA（3、10、30 μM）降低花生四烯酸或膠原蛋白誘導的反應。碩論 Figures 6–9。

Activation markers活化標誌＋

P-selectin expression decreased at 10 and 30 μM UA; PAC-1 binding decreased at 30 μM under the tested conditions. Thesis Figures 10–12.在測試條件下，P-selectin 表現於 UA 10、30 μM 時下降；PAC-1 結合於 30 μM 時下降。碩論 Figures 10–12。

Adhesion, spreading, and clot retraction黏附、伸展與血塊回縮＋

UA at 10 and 30 μM reduced fibrinogen adhesion and spreading and altered clot retraction in the reported assays. Thesis Figures 13–15.在報告的實驗中，UA 10、30 μM 降低纖維蛋白原黏附與伸展，並改變血塊回縮。碩論 Figures 13–15。

Oxidative signal and Akt phosphorylation氧化訊號與 Akt 磷酸化＋

Lucigenin signal and Akt Ser473 phosphorylation were lower with UA treatment. This is an association in the reported experiments, not proof of a complete causal pathway. Thesis Figures 16–19.UA 處理時，lucigenin 訊號與 Akt Ser473 磷酸化較低。這是報告實驗中的關聯，不是完整因果路徑的證明。碩論 Figures 16–19。

Scope研究範圍

These are ex vivo human platelet and platelet-rich plasma experiments. They cannot establish oral effects, clinical efficacy, or safety.以上為離體人類血小板／PRP 實驗，尚不能推論口服效果、臨床療效或安全性。
