Research研究

Biological questions. Clear evidence. Careful claims.生物醫學問題。清楚證據。審慎判斷。

I work in experimental biology and platelet signaling, and I read a lot of literature. Whatever I explain, I try to stay honest about what the data can and can’t support.我做實驗生物學和血小板訊號，也讀很多文獻。不管解釋什麼，我盡量誠實面對資料能支持到哪、不能到哪。

Areas of inquiry研究關注

From molecular signals to defensible mechanism.從分子訊號走向可被證據支持的機制。

01

Platelet biology and signaling血小板生物學與訊號

How extracellular cues, receptors, adhesion pathways, and intracellular signaling shape platelet responses.理解細胞外訊號、受體、黏附路徑與細胞內訊號如何共同形成血小板反應。

02

Bioactive compounds and mechanism生物活性物質與機制

How compound identity, preparation, cell context, and directness of evidence change the strength of a mechanistic claim.化合物身分、製備方式、細胞情境與證據直接性，如何改變機制主張的可信程度。

03

Evidence architecture證據架構

Separating what a paper reports, what can be inferred, and which causal links remain unresolved.區分文獻實際報告、可以合理推論的內容，以及仍未解決的因果連結。

Research practice研究方法

Evidence should stay traceable.每一個判斷都應該能回到證據。

Observe

Start from what the experiment or source actually shows.先看實驗或來源真正顯示什麼。

Verify

Check identity, context, direction, and directness.核對身分、情境、方向與直接性。

Structure

Separate evidence nodes before connecting them.連線之前，先拆清楚證據節點。

Explain

Make uncertainty visible instead of smoothing it away.讓不確定性被看見，而不是被修飾掉。
